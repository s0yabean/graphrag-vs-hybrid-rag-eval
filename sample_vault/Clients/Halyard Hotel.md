# Halyard Hotel

Boutique hotel on the seafront, wholesale client since February 2025. We met their food and beverage manager at the Port Alder Hospitality Expo in November 2024.

They use filter coffee for breakfast service and [[Harbour Blend]] in the bar, about 60 kg a month. [[Sam Patel]] trained their breakfast team.
