# Juniper Cafe Group

Six cafés across Port Alder and Millbrook, and our largest wholesale account since July 2024. Head of purchasing: Owen Brandt.

[[Priya Nandakumar]] made the introduction; see [[2024-06-18 Intro meeting Juniper Cafe Group]]. They started with a 40 kg trial order and by mid-2025 were taking around 180 kg a month of [[Harbour Blend]] and a seasonal single origin.

In January 2026 they renewed for a year at a volume price of $27.00 per kg. Owen has asked for an espresso that works better with oat milk; [[Daniel Reyes]] is testing options.
