# Fernwick at Home

Our coffee subscription for home brewers: two 250 g bags every month, $18 a month, delivered free. Customers choose [[Harbour Blend]], the seasonal single origin, or one of each.

Launched on 4 November 2024, after slipping from the original Q3 2024 target in the [[2024 Plan]]. Subscriber numbers are reported at each board meeting.
