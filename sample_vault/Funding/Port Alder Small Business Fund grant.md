# Port Alder Small Business Fund grant

Grant of $15,000 from the Port Alder Small Business Fund, awarded on 2 October 2025, to fit an afterburner to the roaster. Applied in July 2025; [[Lena Fischer]] wrote most of the application.

The afterburner went in later that month. Smoke complaints from the neighbouring units have stopped.
