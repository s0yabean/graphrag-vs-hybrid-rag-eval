# 2025 Plan

Where we are: 21 wholesale accounts, about 240 [[Fernwick at Home]] subscribers, and the 5 kg roaster close to capacity. We are 8 people.

Goals for 2025:
1. Open a second roastery in Millbrook by Q2 2026, closer to the [[Juniper Cafe Group]] sites. Budget for the fit-out: $250,000.
2. Finance it with a bank loan of $250,000; no outside equity.
3. Reach 30 wholesale accounts and 500 subscribers by December.
4. Start buying at least part of our coffee direct from a producer.
