# 2025 Port Alder Regional Roasting Cup

Regional roasting competition held on 25 October 2025, with 14 roasters entered. [[Daniel Reyes]] placed second in the espresso category for [[Fernwick Roasting Co]], roasting a natural Caturra from [[Cooperativa Rio Claro]].
