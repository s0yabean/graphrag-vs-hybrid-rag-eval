# Never depend on one importer

Learned from the 2025 delays at [[Alta Verde Importers]]: one late container left [[Harbour Blend]] out of stock for four days, and we had no other source of a similar coffee.

Rule: keep at least two sources for every component of the house blend, and hold four weeks of green coffee stock. See [[2025-06 Switch green coffee supplier]].
