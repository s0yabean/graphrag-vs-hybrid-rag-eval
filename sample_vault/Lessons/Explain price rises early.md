# Explain price rises early

Learned from comparing our two price rises. The January 2025 rise came with five weeks' notice and the actual green coffee numbers, and no accounts left. The September 2025 rise came with two weeks' notice by email, and [[The Copper Kettle]] left.

Rule: at least five weeks' notice, the reasons in numbers, and a call to every account over 20 kg a month.
