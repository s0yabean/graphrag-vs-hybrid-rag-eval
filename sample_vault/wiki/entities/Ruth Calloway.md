# Ruth Calloway

Founder and managing director of [[Fernwick Roasting Co]]. These are my notes.

Before Fernwick I spent nine years as a coffee and tea buyer for Brightway Grocers, which is where I learned to cup and to negotiate with importers. I started roasting at weekends in 2019 and opened the Quay Street unit in 2021.

I handle sales, supplier relationships and the plan. [[Lena Fischer]] handles the finances.
