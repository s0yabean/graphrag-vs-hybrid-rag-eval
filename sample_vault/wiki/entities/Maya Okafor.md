# Maya Okafor

Head roaster at [[Fernwick Roasting Co]] from the day we opened in 2021 until the end of April 2025. Maya built our roast profiles for [[Harbour Blend]] and trained [[Daniel Reyes]].

She left at the end of April 2025 to move to Lisbon with her family. We parted on good terms and she still answers the odd question about the old profiles.
