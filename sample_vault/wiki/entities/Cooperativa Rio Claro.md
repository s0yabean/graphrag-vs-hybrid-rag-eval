# Cooperativa Rio Claro

A coffee growers' cooperative in Huila, Colombia, with about 140 member farms. Our export contact is Tomas Herrera.

We buy direct from them since July 2025, when the first container arrived. They supply the Colombian component of [[Harbour Blend]] and our seasonal natural Caturra. See [[2025-06 Switch green coffee supplier]].
