# Alta Verde Importers

Green coffee importer that supplied most of our coffee from 2021 until June 2025. Our account manager was Grace Lindqvist.

Reliable for years, but in 2025 two shipments arrived late (April and May) and they announced an 18% price increase for the second half of the year. We moved our main volume to [[Cooperativa Rio Claro]]. We still buy the Brazilian component of [[Harbour Blend]] from them in small lots.
