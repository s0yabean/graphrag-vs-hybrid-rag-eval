# Fernwick Roasting Co

Fernwick Roasting Co (FRC) is a specialty coffee roaster in Port Alder, founded in 2021 by [[Ruth Calloway]]. We roast in a unit on Quay Street and sell three ways: wholesale to cafés, bakeries and hotels, retail bags through the website, and the [[Fernwick at Home]] subscription.

Our house espresso is [[Harbour Blend]]. Green coffee comes mainly from [[Cooperativa Rio Claro]]; before July 2025 it came from [[Alta Verde Importers]].
