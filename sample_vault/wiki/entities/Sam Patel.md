# Sam Patel

Wholesale support and barista trainer at [[Fernwick Roasting Co]] since September 2023. Sam visits client cafés to dial in grinders and trains their staff on [[Harbour Blend]], and handles equipment questions from wholesale accounts.

Not to be confused with Sam Whitlock, who owns [[Corner Hound Cafe]].
