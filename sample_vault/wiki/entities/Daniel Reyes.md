# Daniel Reyes

Joined [[Fernwick Roasting Co]] in March 2023 as production roaster, trained by [[Maya Okafor]]. Head roaster since 1 May 2025.

Daniel ran the cupping trials that decided the supplier switch to [[Cooperativa Rio Claro]], and placed second at the [[2025 Port Alder Regional Roasting Cup]].
