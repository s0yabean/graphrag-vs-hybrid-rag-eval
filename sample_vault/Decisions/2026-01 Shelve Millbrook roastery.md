# 2026-01 Shelve Millbrook roastery

**Decision:** we will not open the second roastery in Millbrook. Instead we lease a 15 kg roaster for the Quay Street unit. Agreed at the [[2026-01-20 Board meeting Q4 2025]].

**Why:**
- The fit-out quote for the Millbrook site came in at $410,000, against the $250,000 we had budgeted in the [[2025 Plan]].
- The bank offered a loan of only $150,000.
- A 15 kg roaster triples our batch size without a second rent, second team or second set of permits.

**What follows:** five-year lease at $2,100 a month, installation planned for May 2026. See [[Roasting equipment]].
