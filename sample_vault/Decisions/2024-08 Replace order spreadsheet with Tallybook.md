# 2024-08 Replace order spreadsheet with Tallybook

**Decision:** move wholesale ordering from the shared spreadsheet to the [[Tallybook order portal]] by September 2024. Decided by [[Ruth Calloway]] with [[Lena Fischer]].

**Why:** we missed three wholesale orders in July 2024, all from rows that were overwritten in the spreadsheet, and Lena was spending about a day a week matching orders to invoices. With [[Juniper Cafe Group]] coming on, the volume would only grow.

**Cost:** $49 a month.
