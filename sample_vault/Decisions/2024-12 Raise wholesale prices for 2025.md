# 2024-12 Raise wholesale prices for 2025

**Decision:** raise [[Harbour Blend]] from $24.00 to $26.50 per kg from 6 January 2025, and the other coffees by a similar amount. See [[2025-01 Wholesale price list]].

**Why:** our green coffee costs rose 22% year on year in 2024, and we had not changed prices since opening.

**How:** a letter to every wholesale client on 2 December, five weeks ahead, explaining the green coffee market with the actual numbers. [[Sam Patel]] called the larger accounts in person. No accounts left because of it.
