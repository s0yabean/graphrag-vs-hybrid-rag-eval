# 2025-08 Raise Harbour Blend to 28 per kg

**Decision:** [[Harbour Blend]] goes from $26.50 to $28.00 per kg from 1 September 2025. Single origins and decaf rise too. See [[2025-09 Wholesale price list]].

**Why:** the move to [[Cooperativa Rio Claro]] raised our landed coffee cost by about 9% per kg, and we want the price to pay for the direct-trade premium we promised the cooperative.

**How:** an email to clients on 18 August, two weeks ahead. Less notice than last time because the first Rio Claro invoice came in later than expected.
