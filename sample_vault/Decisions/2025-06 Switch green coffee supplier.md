# 2025-06 Switch green coffee supplier

**Decision:** move our main green coffee volume from [[Alta Verde Importers]] to buying direct from [[Cooperativa Rio Claro]], starting with a first container in July 2025. Decided by [[Ruth Calloway]] and [[Daniel Reyes]] on 16 June 2025.

**Why:**
- Alta Verde shipments arrived late in April and May 2025, and [[Harbour Blend]] was out of stock for four days in May.
- Alta Verde announced an 18% price increase for the second half of 2025.
- In Daniel's blind cupping, the Rio Claro washed lot scored 86 points against 83 for the Alta Verde lot it would replace.

**Trade-offs:** Rio Claro coffee still costs us about 9% more per kg landed than Alta Verde did before their increase, and we have to pre-pay half of each container.

**What follows:** a wholesale price review for September, and a direct-trade label on the bags.
